Latency Comparison Numbers (~2012)

How long common operations take on a computer, from a CPU cache hit to a packet crossing the Atlantic and back. Each bar sits on the same logarithmic scale, so every gridline is ten times the one before.

Nanoseconds Microseconds Milliseconds
Operation Latency
0.1 ns 10 ns 1 µs 1 ms 1 s

Notes

1 ns = 10−9 seconds 1 µs = 10−6 seconds = 1,000 ns 1 ms = 10−3 seconds = 1,000 µs = 1,000,000 ns
